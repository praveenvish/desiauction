**Why this:** in the paper auction we studied, the stage kept asking teams how many points
they had left, and read the totals out by hand ("kitne point bacha liye?", "12 point
baaki, 13 point baaki"). The source league, studio, place and people are never named,
shown or clipped. The end card is site copy, word for word: *"No more “bhai, hisaab?”"*
and *"Every squad and what it paid, from the auction's record."*
