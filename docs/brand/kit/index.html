DesiAuction brand kit

The final DA mark: your current logo, unchanged in shape, centred, with hand-drawn 16px and 24px versions, the "strike" motion logo, a Hindi lockup and print-ready CMYK files. Every file in this kit was generated from one source and checked automatically.

The mark

Motion: "the strike"

The slash falls like a gavel, cuts in front of the D, and the tile takes the hit on the same frame as the sting (400 ms). For video with sound, use the MP4s in motion/. motion/da-strike.html plays it live with sound.

Hindi: देसी ऑक्शन

Set in Anek Devanagari 600, the product's own Devanagari font, and shaped with HarfBuzz so the conjunct क्श and every vowel sign are correct. The headline line sits on the English capital height, and the stroke weight matches the English wordmark (measured within 3%). The tagline is खेल यहीं से शुरू. It has no letter-spacing, because spacing Devanagari breaks the headline line.

Print

Vector CMYK PDF and EPS, with a two-colour spot version and a two-page spec sheet for printers (print/brand-print-spec.pdf). The D–slash gap is real empty space in every print file. Black letters print as 100K overprint so a plate shift can't open a white line. Large dark areas use a cool rich black at 230% total ink.

Rules

Do
  • Leave clear space of at least a quarter of the tile's width on every side.
  • Use the 16px version at 16px, the 24px version between 17 and 31px, and the main mark from 32px up.
  • Keep the lockup at least 120px wide; below that, use the mark alone.
  • Over photos, put the mark on an ink scrim or use the one-colour glyph.
  • Play the strike at moments that matter: app open, the live room, intros, share videos.
Don't
  • Recolour the tile, or put gold letters on the gold tile.
  • Rotate, stretch, outline, or add shadows or gradients to the mark.
  • Redraw the A, open its counter, or close the D–slash gap. That gap is the logo.
  • Loop the strike, or play it on static UI such as the header or menus.
  • Autoplay the sting. Sound plays only after a tap, and never for users who've turned off motion.
