# 06 — Brand Guidelines

> Canon: C-1, C-4, C-5, C-24 · v1.0 · 2026-07-11

## Brand core

- **Name:** DesiAuction (C-1). "Desi" is pride, not slang — ours, local, of the community. Written as one word, capital D, capital A in the wordmark context; "DesiAuction" in prose.
- **Promise:** *The night goes right.*
- **Personality:** a great match referee — precise, unflappable, quietly warm, incorruptible. Never a hype-man, never a bureaucrat.

## Voice

Three registers, one personality (details in 20-ux-writing):

| Register | Where | Sounds like |
|----------|-------|-------------|
| **Scorer** (default) | Console, forms, system messages | Plain, present tense, exact numbers. "Pool locked. 64 players, 4 rounds." |
| **Commentator** (restrained) | Stage, Owner Room moments | Energy without exclamation marks. "Rohit Sharma goes to Titans — ₹1.2 L." |
| **Ceremony** | SOLD, champion | The only place for scale. "SOLD" |

Rules: no exclamation marks outside ceremony; no apologies-as-filler ("Oops!" is banned); numbers in Indian notation (C-7); bilingual-ready — every string externalized, English first, Hindi next (C-24).

## Visual identity summary

- Palette: Ink darkness, Chalk paper, **Volt** as the single brand accent, Gold earned only (08).
- Type: Clash Display for moments, Geist Sans for interface, Geist Mono for money (09).
- The brand's signature image is **the lit pitch in darkness**: a field of Ink with one Volt-lit focal area. Marketing, empty states, and the Stage idle screen all derive from this composition.

## Applications

- **Public surfaces (Stage, Overlay, results):** DesiAuction brandmark persistent but small (bottom corner); Free-tier shows "Powered by DesiAuction," Pro shows organizer branding first, ours second (45-billing).
- **Organizer branding:** tournaments can carry the organizer's name, logo, and sponsor strip on public surfaces. Their brand leads on their night; our craft is the signature. Sponsor slots are structured (name + logo + optional strip), never free-form ads.
- **Receipts and exports** carry the brandmark and a verification line — exported artifacts are brand ambassadors and carry provenance (invariant 33).

## What the brand never does

- Never manufactured urgency, countdown theatrics for sales, fake scarcity (invariant 31).
- Never gambling aesthetics: no casino golds-on-red, no slot-machine motion. This is sport and community money.
- Never mock or expose: no unsold jokes, no "cheapest buy" widgets, nothing that spends a person's dignity for engagement (C-23).
- Never English-only assumptions in imagery or copy; the brand is at home in Hindi (C-24).
