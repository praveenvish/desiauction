## 1. The rules every post obeys (from the growth charter — CONFIRMED)

- **Who it's for:** ~60% of posts speak to **organisers** (the buyer), not players.
- **Positioning:** CricHeroes owns the season; nobody owns auction night. We are
  *the night before their season* — never a competitor, never named.
- **Engine:** the **Founding 25** — 25 hand-picked organisers run real auctions
  free in exchange for *written* permission to film. Their nights become the content.
- **The metric:** completed auctions from social-sourced organisers. Not followers.
- **Never:** IPL / BCCI / ICC / franchise marks; betting, odds or fantasy words
  or hashtags; a player's name, face or price without written consent; unsold
  lists or player rankings; a competitor's name; any number not read off a real
  ledger; paid ads before the organic gates; `#desi` alone or "Desi Auction" as
  two words.
- **Compliance:** ASCI disclosure in the caption's first line for any paid or
  barter post; label synthetic imagery (simplest: never make any); contests are
  skill-based with published T&Cs, excluding Tamil Nadu.
- **GATE-1 (sign-up CTAs):** site reachable ✔, crawlers allowed ✔ (robots.txt
  `Allow: /`), share image renders ✔, sign-in works — the site moved from SMS
  OTP to an **email code** (`/login`, checked 2026-10-02). **VERIFY** one real
  sign-in email arrives before week 1's CTA posts.

## 2. Content pillars

| Pillar | Share | What it is | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Run your auction** | 30% | How-tos for organisers: setup in 2 minutes, one WhatsApp registration link, owner invites, the big screen, receipts | Screen-capture Reels, carousels, YouTube how-tos |
| **B. Auction nights** | 25% | Real Founding 25 nights: the room, the big screen, the SOLD moment — with written consent | Reels, YouTube long-form, Stories |
| **C. Proof** | 15% | Why nobody argues: server-verified bids, append-only ledger, numbered receipts, recovery when a phone dies | Ledger cards (T7), short explainers |
| **D. Owner strategy** | 15% | Purse planning, bid ladders, when to stop — useful to the people in the room | Carousels, Threads/X threads |
| **E. The culture** | 15% | Relatable auction-night humour, tag-a-friend — the shareable reach engine | Carousels, memes in brand style |

Pillars A + C + D are the organiser 60%.

**Every claim comes from the site's copy (CONFIRMED in `marketing.ts`):**
"One link registers players", "The server checks every bid, not the loudest
voice", "Dues computed at SOLD, with numbered receipts", "A dead phone stops
nothing", "No more 'bhai, hisaab?'", "Free during beta · always free for up to 4
teams and 40 players", "Tournaments started during beta stay free forever".

## 5. Production

- **Real footage only.** Screen captures: `docs/brand/social-launch/reels/capture-demo.cjs`
  (the live site's demo) and `apps/web/scripts/capture-marketing-screens.ts`
  (a scripted practice auction, fictional names — CONFIRMED in the script).
- **Look:** Clash Display + Geist, ink and gold, the lit-pitch scene
  (`docs/brand/social-launch/build-covers.cjs`, `instagram/build-stories.cjs`).
- **Fictional names stay labelled** ("Simulated demo · fictional players", as the site does).
- **Uploads from the desktop:** H.264 for anything Meta (its web uploaders stall
  on HEVC); 9:16 for Reels/Shorts/Stories; 4:5 for feed carousels.

## 6. What we measure (weekly)

1. Organiser sign-ups by `utm_source` (site analytics).
2. Founding 25 applications and confirmed nights.
3. Saves and shares per post (IG Insights) — saves = organisers bookmarking how-tos.
4. Comments that are real questions → next week's FAQ content.

Followers are reported but never the goal.
