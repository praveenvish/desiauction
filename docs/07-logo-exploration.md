# 07 — Logo Exploration

> Canon: C-1, C-5, C-6 · v1.0 · 2026-07-11 · Status: direction ratified, final artwork pending a design sprint

## Requirements

The mark must: work at 16px (favicon) and 40ft (projector); read in Ink-on-Chalk, Chalk-on-Ink, and Volt contexts; survive one-color print; feel athletic *and* financial; avoid gavel clichés (every competitor uses a gavel) and casino iconography.

## Directions explored

### A — "The Beam" (recommended)

A minimal floodlight beam: a narrow triangle of light descending onto a horizontal baseline, forming a subtle "D" counterform in the negative space. In motion contexts the beam sweeps once, then holds.

- Why it wins: it *is* the design language (05) in one shape — darkness, one lit place. Unique in category; scales cleanly; the beam doubles as a progress/spotlight motif inside the product (the current lot literally stands in the beam).

### B — "The Paddle Tick"

A bid paddle whose face is a checkmark — bid meets verification. Honest to the product but paddle silhouettes read as ping-pong at small sizes; the check is generic SaaS. Rejected as primary; the paddle glyph joins the icon set (12).

### C — "DA Monogram Stump"

Letterforms D+A composed as three stumps with bails. Clever, cricket-proud, but reads as a cricket-scoring brand and boxes us out of future sports (02 H3). Rejected; may be revisited as a cricket-mode sub-brand.

### D — Wordmark-only

Clash Display wordmark, "Desi" in Chalk/Ink and "Auction" in Volt weight contrast. Strong for web headers; insufficient alone for favicon/app-icon. Kept as the lockup's text component.

## Ratified direction

**A (The Beam) + D (wordmark)** as the lockup; Beam alone as the app icon and favicon.

## Usage rules

- Clearspace: one beam-width on all sides. Minimum size 16px (beam), 120px (lockup).
- Colorways: Chalk beam on Ink (primary), Ink on Chalk (documents), Volt beam on Ink (live surfaces only). Gold beam exists in exactly one place: the champion certificate (C-5).
- Never: gradients inside the mark, outlines, rotation, drop shadows, or placement over photography without an Ink scrim.
