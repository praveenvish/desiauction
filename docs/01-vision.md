# 01 — Vision

> Canon: C-1, C-2, C-3, C-23, C-24 · v1.0 · 2026-07-11

## The one-sentence vision

**Every community sports auction in India runs on DesiAuction, because it is the only place where the money is beyond dispute and the night feels like an occasion.**

## The problem, honestly stated

Tens of thousands of local cricket tournaments run player auctions every year — gully tournaments, corporate leagues, district associations. Today they run on WhatsApp messages, Excel sheets, a loud voice, and trust in whoever holds the pen. When ₹2 lakh of purse moves in an evening:

- **Disputes are normal.** "That bid was mine." "Your sheet says ₹80,000, mine says ₹85,000." The organizer spends the next week defending arithmetic.
- **The occasion is squandered.** A player auction is the most exciting night of a local season — and it looks like a spreadsheet projected on a wall.
- **The organizer suffers most.** The person doing the most work has the worst evening: juggling bids, budgets, squad limits, and accusations simultaneously.

Existing auction apps treat this as a data-entry problem. It is a **trust and theatre** problem.

## What we are building

The trusted operating system for community sports auctions: a platform where

1. **Money is physics, not opinion.** Every bid is server-validated, immutably recorded, and instantly identical on every screen in the room (C-3, C-9). There is nothing to dispute because there is only one truth.
2. **The night is an occasion.** The SOLD moment is a synchronized ceremony across the projector, every owner's phone, and every spectator's screen. Players hear their names called into a moment that looks like television.
3. **The organizer exhales.** Setup is guided, the live conduct surface makes one person calmly powerful, and when it ends, receipts and squads are already done.

Auction first; the season (fixtures, results, standings) follows as the retention layer — but the auction is the wedge and must be the best in the world at its job.

## What winning looks like

- **North Star: Trusted Auctions Completed** (C-2) — completed auctions with zero disputes raised and zero silent failures. We do not celebrate signups; we celebrate finished, undisputed nights.
- Organizers repeat: retention is measured tournament-over-tournament, and the second purchase is the true conversion.
- The phrase we build backwards from: *"For the first time, I actually enjoyed running my own auction."*
- Five-second first impression on any surface: *"this is an exceptional premium product"* — because in this market, looking serious **is** trust.

## What we are not

- Not a fantasy or gambling product. Real teams, real players, community money.
- Not a social network. No feeds, no engagement mechanics, no dark patterns (C-19, invariant 31).
- Not a per-seat enterprise SaaS. Organizers buy a Pass per tournament (C-11); pricing is public and honest.
- Not an AI-driven auctioneer. AI assists at the edges and never touches money (C-10).

## Horizon

1. **H1 — The definitive auction night** (this rebuild): six Golden Journeys, flawless.
2. **H2 — The season**: fixtures, results, standings; the tournament lives on the platform between auctions.
3. **H3 — The ecosystem**: associations running dozens of tournaments, public APIs (C-14), the player's cross-season history — trust compounding into the system of record for community cricket.
