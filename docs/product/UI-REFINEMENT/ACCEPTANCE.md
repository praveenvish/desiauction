# Interface external acceptance register

Local implementation is authorised. These genuine external evidence items remain pending and cannot be replaced by automated checks or agent review.

| Item | Scope and required evidence | Status | Responsible input |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Human prototype study | Six to eight relevant participants; real per-role/task attempts, counterbalanced comparison, errors/time/comprehension and revised designs | PENDING | Product owner supplies participants; implementer prepares prototype/tasks/report |
| Product design review | Concrete annotated six-screen journeys and subsequent waves, with accepted changes and concerns recorded | PENDING | Product owner reviews concrete artifacts |
| Manager/finance/operator studies | Relevant people perform critical tasks; scope, confirmation and recovery understood; no critical error | PENDING | Participants appropriate to each actual responsibility |
| Physical mobile devices | Actual modest Android and iOS Safari; outdoor glare, interrupted connectivity, safe-area/keyboard, text scaling and real passkeys where relevant | PENDING | Device availability and recorded versions/conditions |
| Manual assistive-technology acceptance | Actual screen-reader interactions across critical role journeys, alongside available keyboard/automated checks | PENDING | Appropriate assistive-technology environment and review |
| Controlled real-user pilot | Approved audience, stable treatment, monitored errors, valid rollback and real adoption observations | PENDING | Separate release/pilot authorisation and participants |
| Field performance and conversion | Actual traffic with measurement denominators/conditions; sufficient samples and prespecified experiment analysis | PENDING | Post-release measurement; no local proxy claim |

Available engineering checks must still run in the relevant package. A critical defect blocks Build even when discovered during external acceptance. Missing external evidence is reported precisely at final local handover; it does not authorise release or a claim of universal accessibility or conversion success.
